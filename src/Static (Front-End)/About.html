About Us

Who We Are

Welcome to LabMate — your trusted assistant for booking university computer labs quickly, fairly, and efficiently. Designed by students, for students, LabMate ensures that access to limited lab resources is organized based on your faculty, your course, and your needs.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to give every student fair access to the computing resources they need, without the hassle. Whether you're a design student needing high-end machines or a CS major prepping for a coding exam, LabMate ensures you never miss a lab session again.

Key Features

  • Access labs when you truly need them.
  • Priority based on course requirements and software needs.
  • Fair and transparent booking across all departments.
